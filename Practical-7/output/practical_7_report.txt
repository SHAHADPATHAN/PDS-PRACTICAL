PRACTICAL 7 - DATA WRANGLING
============================================================

Objective:
To aggregate, reshape and filter the balanced dataset for deeper analysis.

1. IP-wise Attack Frequency
The dataset was grouped by Client-IP-address to calculate total requests and attack frequency.

2. Time-Series Analysis
Traffic was resampled into hourly and daily time intervals.

3. Pivot Table
A pivot table was created between category_type and label when category_type was available.

4. Bot Filtering
Bot traffic was identified using the is_bot field or User-Agent patterns.

5. Internal IP Filtering
Private IPv4 ranges such as 10.x.x.x, 172.16.x.x-172.31.x.x and 192.168.x.x were filtered out.

Conclusion:
The balanced dataset was successfully transformed into aggregated and filtered datasets suitable for deeper traffic and attack analysis.
